title: "Machine Learning-Assisted Causal Inference"
excerpt: "Randomization tests for missing outcomes with finite-population-exact Type-I error control."
collection: portfolio
---

Developed imputation-and-re-imputation tests that refit imputers under each treatment assignment. The method uses covariates while retaining valid randomization inference under the proposed missingness mechanism, even when the imputation model is misspecified.

[Read the JASA paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01621459.2025.2516204).
